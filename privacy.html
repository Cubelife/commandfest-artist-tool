Privacy policy

Commandfest Melbourne 2026 Artist Tool · Last updated 22 November 2026

This tool helps you plan which Magic: The Gathering cards to bring to artist signings at Commandfest Melbourne 2026, and tick them off as they're signed.

Using it without an account

If you don't sign in, your collection is stored only in your own browser on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

What we store when you sign in

  • Your sign-in details from Google, Discord or your email: your email address, and your name and profile picture if the service provides them.
  • Your collection: which cards you've added, how many copies, and which copies are signed.

We use this only to sign you in and keep your collection in sync across your devices. We don't sell it, share it with anyone else, or use it for advertising.

Where it's kept

Accounts and collections are stored with Supabase, which provides the sign-in and database service. Each person's collection can only be read and changed by that person. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

"Clear collection" in the tool removes your saved cards. To have your account deleted entirely, email scott.annable@gmail.com (scott.annable@gmail.com) and it will be removed.

Card details and images come from Scryfall. Magic: The Gathering is a trademark of Wizards of the Coast. This tool isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast, Scryfall or Commandfest.
